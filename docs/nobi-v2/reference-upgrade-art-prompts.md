# Imagegen 最终提示词

生成方式：内置 imagegen 工具。用于游戏大厅与入口的背景素材，生成图不包含交互控件或文字。

## lobby

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background artwork for the Nobi Chinese casual game lobby. Create a polished cinematic illustration of a traditional Chinese waterside town at blue hour: layered dark blue mountains, elegant pagoda and pavilions on the right, warm golden lanterns reflected in calm water, mist, subtle stars, navy sky. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. Reserve the left half as quiet dark blue mist and water for live UI text. Palette deep midnight navy, royal blue, small warm gold accents, consistent with an upscale mobile board/card game. No people, no words, no letters, no logos, no user interface, no borders. Detailed painterly game environment, refined rather than cartoonish.

## guandan

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide game cover background for a Chinese Guandan card game. Cinematic realistic 3D game art: close view across an emerald green felt card table with richly varnished walnut wooden rim, warm Chinese tea room in soft focus, golden evening light, wooden chairs, elegant restrained atmosphere. Empty felt surface, NO playing cards, no chips, no people, no words, no logo, no border, no UI. Wide 3:2 crop, table dominates the lower two thirds, quieter dark area on left for UI title and button, warm inviting polished quality.

## go

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide game cover for Go / weiqi. Cinematic photorealistic 3D game art, beautiful thick honey-colored wood Go board with precisely drawn square grid and glossy black and ivory white stones in a naturally scattered midgame position. Oblique close angle, small wooden bowls in background, dark walnut tea room, warm amber light from right, detailed wood grain and soft depth of field. Board and stones primarily on right half, left half dark quiet wood for live UI copy. Wide 3:2 crop. No people, no text, no logos, no user interface.
